Capstone: answer the questions

Five realistic questions from a manager. Answer each one with your dashboard.

Show steps for:

Capstone: this is what the job actually looks like. A manager sends questions, and you answer them with your data, clearly and correctly. No step-by-step instructions this week, just hints if you need them. You have every skill required.

Email from: Program Director, Riverside Community Services

Hi Laura, the board meets next week and I need a few numbers for April 2025 to August 2026:

  1. Which staff member is associated with the highest total spend?
  2. What's our average spend per client in Mental Health?
  3. What percentage of all services have no direct cost ($0)?
  4. Which region has the highest average spend per client?
  5. How many clients have had more than $2,000 spent on them?

A short summary would be great too. Thanks!

This week's checklist

Answer the questions 60 min

Q1. WorkerID (or name) with the highest total spend

Hint for Q1

Table visual: Workers[WorkerID], Workers[Name], Workers[Role], Total Spend, sorted descending. Then think: look at their role. Does high spend mean this person is doing something wrong, or that their role delivers expensive services (like rent supplements)? Good analysts always add that context.

Looker Studio table on Casework Model: dimensions WorkerID, Name, Role; metric Total Spend; sort descending. Then think: look at their role. Does high spend mean this person is doing something wrong, or that their role delivers expensive services (like rent supplements)?

Q2. Average spend per client in Mental Health

Hint for Q2

Your Avg Spend per Client measure with the Program slicer set to Mental Health. Remember to clear other slicers.

Your Avg Spend per Client scorecard with the Program control set to Mental Health. Clear other controls first. (Sheets: on your Client Spend tab, =AVERAGEIFS(B:B, E:E, "Mental Health").)

Q3. Percentage of services with $0 cost (1 decimal)

Hint for Q3

New measure:

% Zero-Cost Services =
DIVIDE(
    CALCULATE([Total Services], ServiceCodes[UnitCost] = 0),
    [Total Services]
)

Format as Percentage. (Excel route: =COUNTIF(Services[UnitCost], 0) / ROWS(Services).)

Chart-level calculated field in Looker Studio, type Percent:

SUM(CASE WHEN UnitCost = 0 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) / COUNT(ServiceID)

(Sheets: =COUNTIF(Services!G2:G, 0) / COUNTA(Services!A2:A).)

Q4. Region with the highest average spend per client

Hint for Q4

Table visual: Clients[Region] and Avg Spend per Client, sorted descending. Compare with which region has the highest total spend. Are they the same? Why might that be?

Table on Casework Model: dimension Region, metric Avg Spend per Client, sorted descending. Compare with which region has the highest total spend. Are they the same? Why might that be?

Q5. Number of clients with total spend over $2,000

Hint for Q5

Easiest in Excel: on your Week 5 Client Spend sheet, =COUNTIF(B:B, ">2000"). In Power BI, a measure:

Clients Over 2000 =
COUNTROWS(FILTER(VALUES(Services[ClientID]), [Total Spend] > 2000))

Easiest in Sheets: on your Week 5 Client Spend tab, =COUNTIF(B:B, ">2000"). In Looker Studio: a table with ClientID and Total Spend, then add a chart Filter: Include → Total Spend → Greater than → 2000. The row count shows at the bottom-right of the table.

Write the summary 20 min

Write 5 sentences to the director in plain language. A good structure:

  1. The headline: total spend, clients served and average per client.
  2. Where the money goes (top program and top category).
  3. One trend over time.
  4. One thing that surprised you, with context (e.g. Q1 or Q4).
  5. One recommendation or follow-up question.

Portfolio tip: save the dashboard screenshot and this summary together in a folder called Portfolio. If you ever apply for a role involving data, this is concrete evidence of what you can do. (The data is fictional, so it's safe to share.)
