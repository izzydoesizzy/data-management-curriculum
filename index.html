A 16-week plan for Laura

From raw data to the report you actually wanted

You already know the questions: How much have we spent per client? Which services cost the most? Who's getting what? This plan teaches you the tools to answer them yourself in Excel and Power BI, one short session a week.

How this works

⏱ 1–2 hours a week

Each week has one main session (60–90 min) and an optional stretch task. Book it in your calendar like an appointment. Same day and time each week works best.

🗂 One dataset, start to finish

You'll work with a fictional agency's casework export: 300 clients, 13,000+ services and a cost per service code. It's the same shape as the data you dealt with at work.

✅ Check your own work

Every week ends with checkpoints, exact questions with exact answers. Type your answer and the page tells you if you got it right. No guessing whether you "did it properly".

💸 Free tools, on a Mac

Everything works with free or already-owned software. See the free toolkit for which Power BI option fits a Mac.

The big question we're building toward: a client has been with the agency for six months. They saw a community help worker 10 times, and each visit had a cost attached. How much did we spend on them, and how does that compare across programs, regions and workers? By Week 5 you'll answer it in Excel. By Week 14 you'll have a dashboard that answers it for every client with one click.

Your 16-week schedule

Phase 1 · Excel foundations

Turn a messy system export into answers: clean it, look things up, total it, pivot it.

  • W1 Set up and meet your data Get your free tools working, download the practice data and learn what a good raw table looks like. · 60 min
  • W2 Tables, sorting and filtering Turn plain cells into an Excel Table and answer your first questions with sort and filter. · 60 min
  • W3 Cleaning messy exports Fix the mess that real systems produce: stray spaces, inconsistent case, duplicates and blanks. · 75 min
  • W4 Lookups: attach the dollar amounts Use XLOOKUP to bring each service's cost into your services table. This is where the money appears. · 60 min
  • W5 Project 1: spend per client Answer the question that started all this: how much have we spent on each client? · 90 min
  • W6 Project 2: PivotTables Summarize 13,000 rows in seconds by program, category and client, with clickable filters. · 90 min

Phase 2 · Power Query

Record your cleaning steps once, then refresh them every month with one click.

  • W7 Power Query: a recipe for cleaning Redo Week 3's cleaning as recorded steps you never have to repeat by hand. · 75 min
  • W8 Merge, append and refresh Join tables inside Power Query and add next month's data without starting over. · 90 min

Phase 3 · Power BI

Connect tables with relationships, write measures, and build the report you always wanted.

  • W9 Get Power BI running on your Mac Choose your Power BI path, load the four tables and find your way around. · 75 min
  • W10 Relationships: connect the dots Link clients, services, codes and workers so Power BI understands how they relate. · 60 min
  • W11 Measures: your first DAX Write the three measures every casework report needs: Total Spend, Clients Served and Avg Spend per Client. · 75 min
  • W12 Time: months, trends and length of stay Add a month column, see spending over time and calculate average length of stay. · 75 min
  • W13 Designing a report people actually use Learn the handful of design rules that separate useful reports from terrible ones. · 60 min
  • W14 Project 3: Casework Cost Dashboard Build a one-page dashboard to an exact spec, then use it to answer questions. · 2 × 60 min

Phase 4 · Capstone

Prove it: answer real questions with your own dashboard, then plan for real data.

  • W15 Capstone: answer the questions Five realistic questions from a manager. Answer each one with your dashboard. · 90 min
  • W16 Real data, safely, and what's next Plan how to get real exports at work, protect client privacy and keep growing. · 60 min

Ground rules that make this stick

  • Don't skip the checkpoints. They're where the learning actually happens.
  • Fall behind? Use the catch-up files. The practice data page has a clean version of the data, so you can jump into any week fresh.
  • Missed a week? Just continue. No need to restart. The schedule moves with you.
  • Stuck for more than 15 minutes? Open the "Stuck?" box on that week, then move on and come back. Getting stuck is normal; staying stuck isn't useful.
