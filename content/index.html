How this works

⏱ 1–2 hours a week

Each week has one main session (60–90 min) and an optional stretch task. Book it in your calendar like an appointment. Same day and time each week works best.

🗂 One dataset, start to finish

You'll work with a fictional agency's casework export: 300 clients, 13,000+ services and a cost per service code. It's the same shape as the data you dealt with at work.

✅ Check your own work

Every week ends with checkpoints, exact questions with exact answers. Type your answer and the page tells you if you got it right. No guessing whether you "did it properly".

💸 Free tools, on a Mac

Everything works with free or already-owned software. See the free toolkit for which Power BI option fits a Mac.

The big question we're building toward: a client has been with the agency for six months. They saw a community help worker 10 times, and each visit had a cost attached. How much did we spend on them, and how does that compare across programs, regions and workers? By Week 5 you'll answer it in Excel. By Week 14 you'll have a dashboard that answers it for every client with one click.

🗂 Built for CaseWORKS. The practice data mirrors what a case-management system like CaseWORKS records. After Week 14, the Caseworks track adds three projects (waitlist, no-shows, caseloads), a library of 12 report recipes, and a list of exactly what to ask your CaseWORKS administrator.

Ground rules that make this stick

  • Don't skip the checkpoints. They're where the learning actually happens.
  • Fall behind? Use the catch-up files. The practice data page has a clean version of the data, so you can jump into any week fresh.
  • Missed a week? Just continue. No need to restart. The schedule moves with you.
  • Stuck for more than 15 minutes? Open the "Stuck?" box on that week, then move on and come back. Getting stuck is normal; staying stuck isn't useful.
