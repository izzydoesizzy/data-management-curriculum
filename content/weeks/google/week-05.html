1. Learn 15 min

The "IFS" family adds up, counts or averages only the rows that match a condition:

=SUMIFS(   what to add up,     where to check,     what to match )
=SUMIFS(   Services!G:G,       Services!B:B,       "C-0042" )

"Add up column G (UnitCost), but only for rows where column B (ClientID) is C-0042." COUNTIFS counts matching rows and AVERAGEIFS averages them. The "S" means you can add more conditions.

Read: SUMIFS function (Google).

Google Sheets version of the spec: same five columns, on a new tab called Client Spend in your Casework Practice spreadsheet.

2. Do 60 min

  1. New tab. Click + at the bottom-left. Rename it Client Spend. Type the five headers from the spec into A1:E1.
  2. List every client once. In A2: 
    =SORT(UNIQUE(FILTER(Services!B2:B, Services!B2:B <> "")))
    The list fills down automatically: one row per client who had services.
  3. Total Spend. In B2: 
    =SUMIFS(Services!G:G, Services!B:B, A2)
    Then double-click the small blue square at the bottom-right corner of B2 to copy it down the list (or drag it down).
  4. Services. In C2: =COUNTIFS(Services!B:B, A2), then fill down.
  5. Avg Cost per Service. In D2: =AVERAGEIFS(Services!G:G, Services!B:B, A2), then fill down.
  6. Program. In E2: =XLOOKUP(A2, Clients!A:A, Clients!D:D), then fill down.
  7. Format columns B and D as currency (Format → Number → Currency). Bold the header row and freeze it (View → Freeze → 1 row).
  8. Overall average. In G2 type =AVERAGE(Services!G2:G). That's the average cost of a service across everyone.
  9. Find the checkpoint clients with ⌘ + F.
Stuck? Try this
  • #REF! in A2 ("would overwrite data"): clear the cells below A2.
  • Every total is $0: UnitCost is text. Check the Week 4 formula returns numbers (right-aligned).
  • Totals are a bit too high: duplicates are still there. Check you have 13,412 service rows.
  • Avg cost per service is off? Average Services!G2:G (all services), not the client averages in column D.
