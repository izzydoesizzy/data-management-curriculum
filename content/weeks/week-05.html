Project 1, the question that started it all: "Per client, how much money have we spent?" Today you build a client spend summary: one row per client with their total spend, number of services and average cost per service.

Project spec (exact parameters)

Deliverable: a new sheet called Client Spend in Casework Practice.xlsx with these columns:

ColumnHeaderContents
AClientIDEvery client who received at least one service, A→Z
BTotal SpendSum of UnitCost for that client (currency)
CServicesNumber of services for that client
DAvg Cost per ServiceAverage UnitCost for that client (currency)
EProgramThe client's program (from the Clients table)

Data: the cleaned Services table with UnitCost from Week 4.

This week's checklist

  • Create the Client Spend sheet with a unique list of clients
  • Add Total Spend with SUMIFS
  • Add Services with COUNTIFS and Avg Cost per Service with AVERAGEIFS
  • Add Program with XLOOKUP
  • Answer all five checkpoints

1. Learn 15 min

The "IFS" family adds up, counts or averages only the rows that match a condition:

=SUMIFS(   what to add up,       where to check,       what to match )
=SUMIFS(   Services[UnitCost],   Services[ClientID],   "C-0042" )

"Add up UnitCost, but only for rows where ClientID is C-0042." COUNTIFS counts matching rows and AVERAGEIFS averages them. The "S" means you can add more conditions, e.g. only C-0042 and only Financial Assistance.

Read: SUMIFS function.

2. Do 60 min

  1. New sheet. Click the + next to the sheet tabs. Rename it Client Spend. Type the five headers from the spec into A1:E1.
  2. List every client once. In A2 type: 
    =SORT(UNIQUE(Services[ClientID]))
    The list "spills" down automatically: one row per client who had services.
  3. Total Spend. In B2: 
    =SUMIFS(Services[UnitCost], Services[ClientID], A2)
    Then double-click the small square at the bottom-right corner of B2 (the fill handle) to copy it down to the end of the list.
  4. Services. In C2: =COUNTIFS(Services[ClientID], A2), then fill down.
  5. Avg Cost per Service. In D2: =AVERAGEIFS(Services[UnitCost], Services[ClientID], A2), then fill down.
  6. Program. In E2: =XLOOKUP(A2, Clients[ClientID], Clients[Program]), then fill down.
  7. Format columns B and D as Currency. Make the header row bold. Freeze the top row.
  8. Overall average. In G2 type =AVERAGE(Services[UnitCost]). That's the average cost of a service across everyone.
  9. Find the checkpoint clients with ⌘ + F. Save.

3. Checkpoint 10 min

Total spend for client C-0042?

Total spend for client C-0117?

Total spend for client C-0250?

How many services did C-0042 receive?

What is the average cost per service across all services? (round to cents)

Stuck? Try this
  • #SPILL! in A2: something is in the way below A2. Clear cells A3 downward.
  • Every total is $0: the UnitCost column contains text, not numbers. Check that the Week 4 XLOOKUP returns numbers (they line up on the right of the cell).
  • Totals are a bit too high: duplicates are still in your data. Check your row count is 13,412.
  • Avg cost per service is off by a cent or two? Make sure you averaged Services[UnitCost] (all services), not the client averages in column D. Those are different questions!

Stretch (optional, 20 min)

  • Select B2 downward → Home → Conditional Formatting → Data Bars. The biggest spenders jump out.
  • How many clients have spent more than $5,000? =COUNTIF(B:B, ">5000")
