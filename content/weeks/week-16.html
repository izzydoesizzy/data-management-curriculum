1. Real client data: the rules 20 min

Casework data is some of the most sensitive data there is. Before you touch real data:

  1. Follow your organization's privacy policy first. Ask your privacy officer or manager what you're allowed to access and where it may be stored. In Canada, health and social service data is covered by laws like PHIPA (Ontario) and PIPEDA; in the US, HIPAA may apply.
  2. Ask only for what the question needs. To calculate spend per client you need ClientID, ServiceCode, ServiceDate and cost. You don't need names, birthdates, addresses or case notes.
  3. De-identify. Use system IDs instead of names. Use age bands instead of dates of birth. Never include free-text notes.
  4. Watch small numbers. "1 client in the West region aged 65+ in Newcomer Settlement" can identify someone. Many agencies hide counts under 5 in shared reports.
  5. Keep it on approved systems. Use work laptops and approved cloud storage. Never use a personal Power BI account or personal email for real client data.

2. Ask for an export 15 min

Most case-management systems can export to CSV or Excel. In CaseWORKS that usually means an ad-hoc or custom report exported to Excel; your CaseWORKS administrator can tell you what's available. The Caseworks page has a checklist of questions to ask. Copy and adapt this:

Subject: Data export request: service costs by client (de-identified)

Hi [name],

For a cost analysis of [program], could I get a CSV export covering
[start date] to [end date] with these fields only:

  Services:   ServiceID, ClientID, ServiceDate, ServiceCode, WorkerID
  Clients:    ClientID, Program, Region, IntakeDate, DischargeDate
  Codes:      ServiceCode, Description, Category, UnitCost

No names, dates of birth, contact details or case notes needed.
I'll keep the file on [approved location] and delete it when done.

Ideally this could be a saved export I can re-run monthly.

Thanks,
Laura

Notice it has the same shape as the practice data. That's on purpose: everything you built will work on it.
